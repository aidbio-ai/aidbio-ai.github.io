Capabilities

Methods shaped by the biological question.

For research groups and R&D teams working with molecular data, field observations, and repeated scientific analyses.

01 / FIELD

Agriculture and climate

Connect field observations with weather history to examine variation across sites, seasons, and treatments.

Possible outputs: Documented climate tables, site comparisons, and inspectable reports.

02 / MOLECULAR

Gene expression and regulation

Analyze expression patterns, interpret gene sets, and investigate regulatory hypotheses with documented methods and experimental context.

Possible outputs: Expression and enrichment figures, gene lists, reviewed comparisons, and methods.

03 / MODELS

Protein networks and predictive models

Represent biological relationships explicitly and evaluate predictions against baselines and suitable held-out data.

Possible outputs: Baselines, predictions, evaluation protocols, and explicit limitations.

04 / WORKFLOWS

Scientific software and reporting

Turn repeated analyses into documented workflows, figures, reports, and maintainable tools. Keep generated interpretation tied to the underlying evidence.

Possible outputs: Documented code, figures, reports, and a reproducible handover.

Evaluation and interpretation

Test the question you intend to answer.

Replicates, related proteins, sites, seasons, and batch structure can change what an evaluation means. The split and comparison must match the intended use.

Research analysis

Review the design, contrasts, missingness, and inferential scope. Separate exploratory patterns from supported statistical conclusions.

Predictive modeling

Choose baselines and held-out groups appropriate to the task. Report uncertainty, failure cases, and the limits of generalization.
