Solutions

What we can do for your data.

We work with research groups and with companies. The questions differ, but the approach is the same: understand the data first, test honestly, and hand over something you can run yourself.

For research groups

Analysis you can publish

  • Statistics, machine learning and figures for a paper or thesis.
  • Methods written so reviewers can follow them.
  • Code and data kept organised and repeatable.
For companies

Pipelines your team can run

  • From pilot data to a working, documented pipeline.
  • Models tested the way you will use them.
  • A clear handover to your engineers.

Areas we work in

Agriculture and climate

Field trials and weather

Combine trial data with location-based weather history, compute measures such as growing-degree days, and compare treatments across sites and seasons.

Omics and bioinformatics

Gene expression and proteins

Differential expression, pathway enrichment, dimensionality reduction, and models of proteins and their interactions.

Machine learning

Models that hold up

Prediction and explanation for lab, field and manufacturing data, with validation that matches real use. For example, whole fields or experiments are held out, not random rows.

Reports and automation

Less manual reporting

AI-assisted drafting of figure legends and reports from your analysis outputs, run locally so your data stays with you.
