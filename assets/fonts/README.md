# Site fonts

Inter and JetBrains Mono are the established self-hosted site fonts. The October 2026 blue/orange marketing refresh also uses self-hosted Baloo 2 and Plus Jakarta Sans for the approved softer mock typography. The latter are WOFF2 Latin and Latin Extended subsets served locally; system fonts remain CSS fallbacks.

Baloo 2 and Plus Jakarta Sans came from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. Their license texts are saved alongside the files. The four public WOFF2 sources are:

- Baloo 2 Latin: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/baloo2/v23/wXKrE3kTposypRyd51jcAA.woff2
- Baloo 2 Latin Extended: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/baloo2/v23/wXKrE3kTposypRyd51bcANwr.woff2
- Plus Jakarta Sans Latin: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/plusjakartasans/v12/LDIoaomQNQcsA88c7O9yZ4KMCoOg4Ko20yw.woff2
- Plus Jakarta Sans Latin Extended: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/plusjakartasans/v12/LDIoaomQNQcsA88c7O9yZ4KMCoOg4Ko40yyygA.woff2
