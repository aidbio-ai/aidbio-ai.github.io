gPPIpred: predicting protein interactions

Proteins work by binding to each other, but testing which ones do is slow and costly in the lab. gPPIpred predicts interactions from protein structure, and points to the residues likely to be involved.

  • Each protein is turned into a graph that keeps its 3D shape and chemical properties.
  • A graph neural network with attention scores the pair and highlights key residues, so predictions can be inspected.
  • Trained and validated on curated structural datasets, with a pipeline for large-scale screening.
Read the paper →

Matiolli CC, Marques J, Abreu IA. gPPIpred: A User-Friendly PPI Predictor Based on Protein Molecular Graphs. 2026.

How it works
  1. Fetch the two protein structures.
  2. Build a molecular graph for each.
  3. Score the pair with a graph neural network.
  4. Report the prediction and the residues behind it.
