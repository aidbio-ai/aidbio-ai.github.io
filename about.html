About AidBio

Why AidBio exists.

Research data rarely arrive as one clean story. Experiments, field observations, published knowledge, and computational results often live apart, even when one question needs all of them.

Bring the pieces into focus.

AidBio connects biological context, careful analysis, and reusable software. The goal is work a team can inspect, question, and build on: clear figures, traceable evidence, and methods that travel with the result.

That approach grew from Cleverson Matiolli, PhD's work across experimental plant biology, biological networks, computational modeling, and scientific software. The founder story follows this trajectory through primary literature and the applications that grew from those questions.
