# Figure rights

No figures from third-party publications are reproduced or hot-linked
anywhere in this portfolio.

Every diagram on the six chapter pages is an original, inline SVG created
for this site to illustrate a concept described in the surrounding text
(e.g. two signals converging on a transcription factor, a small node graph,
a data cube collapsing into a time series). Each is labelled in its
figcaption as:

> Schematic — original diagram, not a reproduction of a published figure.

Rationale: reliably determining the redistribution licence of a specific
figure from a specific paper is not something that could be verified with
confidence in this session, so — per the narrative-integrity rules in
`../AGENTS.md` — original schematic diagrams were used instead of remote
paper imagery. This also removes any dependency on a third-party image host
being reachable, so the pages render identically online and offline.

If a real, appropriately-licensed paper figure should be added later:

1. Confirm the article and the specific figure are published under a
   licence that permits redistribution with attribution (e.g. CC BY).
2. Record the licence, source URL and attribution text in this file next
   to the relevant chapter.
3. Serve the image from this repository (not hot-linked from the
   publisher) with descriptive `alt` text, and keep a text-only fallback
   for cases where the image fails to load.
