2026 · gPPIpred

Return to the protein-interaction question — now with graphs.

Years after experimental Y2H work, gPPIpred addresses the same biological class of question computationally. Proteins are represented as molecular graphs whose nodes are amino-acid residues and whose edges encode spatial proximity. A GATv2-based Siamese network predicts whether pairs interact and uses saliency/attention to identify candidate hotspot residues.

The independent validation set contained 72,358 protein pairs, and the paper reports MCC 0.4641 with sensitivity of 96% at the selected threshold.
