Chapter 02 · Complexity becomes a network

One regulator became many interacting parts.

Protein interaction studies changed the unit of reasoning. Instead of asking only what one gene does, the question became: what structure emerges from many biological relationships?

UC Davis · 2017–2019

Experimental interaction maps are graphs in disguise.

At UC Davis, work on a comprehensive Arabidopsis yeast-two-hybrid resource placed protein–protein interactions at the center of the research. The experimental vocabulary was Y2H, bait, prey and validation. The computational vocabulary is nodes, edges, degree, neighborhoods and graph structure.

That conceptual translation is crucial to the later move into graph machine learning.
