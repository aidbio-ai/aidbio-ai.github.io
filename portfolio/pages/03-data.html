Complexity eventually exceeds manual reasoning.

The computational turn

Computation was a response to scale.

Transcriptomics

RNA-seq converts molecular response into large expression matrices that can be compared across genotypes, treatments, tissues and time.

Phenotyping

Python/OpenCV workflows at ITQB NOVA reduced manual image-processing effort by approximately 80%, turning images into quantitative plant traits at experiment scale.

Field data

Spreadsheets, developmental stages and longitudinal plant observations require identity tracking, normalization and reproducible ETL before statistical analysis is meaningful.

From “data cleaning” to scientific identity.

A core engineering lesson is that biological observations need stable identity across time. The public excelxtract project formalizes that idea by converting multi-sheet phenology workbooks to tidy, ML-ready data and generating persistent sample IDs for specific plant units.

raw workbook → sheet classification → wide-to-long normalization → stable sample identity → PCA / t-SNE → interpretable developmental patterns

Data is not evidence until provenance survives the transformation.

This becomes one of AidBio’s strongest design principles: every stage should preserve enough metadata to reconstruct what was measured, how it was transformed and how a conclusion was produced.
