AidBio is the synthesis.

Not a switch from biology to technology, but a convergence: molecular biology, agronomy, bioinformatics, machine learning, scientific software, environmental analytics and AI engineering assembled around one method of reasoning.

The operating model

Transform biological complexity into decisive action.

Biology

Mechanisms, experimental design, molecular genetics, RNA biology, protein interactions, stress physiology and genotype-to-phenotype reasoning.

Computation

Bioinformatics, scientific Python/R, graph learning, computer vision, statistical modeling, climate data and reproducible pipelines.

Decision systems

Evidence-led visualization, reports, local-first LLM workflows, agents and tools that preserve provenance rather than replacing expert judgment.

What AidBio builds

AidBio develops scientific software, bioinformatics pipelines, machine-learning systems, climate analytics, computer-vision workflows and AI-assisted tools for agriculture, biotechnology, plant science and applied R&D.

The goal is not generic automation. The goal is to encode scientific reasoning into repeatable systems that make complex evidence easier to inspect, challenge and use.

The founder journey can be read as a progressive expansion of scientific scale: from understanding how one transcription factor integrates sugar and stress signals, to building computational systems intended to integrate molecular, biological, environmental and agronomic information.
