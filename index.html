STAGE 01 · BIOLOGICAL FOUNDATIONBottom-Left Green Arc & Inner Leaf

Experiment Design

Define research questions, experimental structures, sampling strategies, and data collection methods for laboratory or field studies.

Key capabilities & outputs · click to inspect

Factorial & Block Designs: Randomized complete block (RCBD) and split-plot structures to separate treatment effects from spatial field gradients or laboratory plate effects.

Statistical Power & Sample Size: A priori power simulations to assess biological and technical replication for a target effect and planned testing criteria.

Sampling & Metadata Protocols: Structured collection protocols and metadata connecting phenotype, environmental observations and time-series sampling to downstream analyses.

Confounding & Batch Mitigation: Balance treatments across extraction runs, sequencing lanes and greenhouse blocks; review confounding and effect identifiability.

STAGE 02 · RIGOROUS COMPUTATIONTop-Left Teal-to-Royal Blue Arc

Data Processing & Statistical Analysis

Transform raw experimental and high-throughput biological measurements through rigorous quality control, normalization, and statistical modeling within reproducible pipelines.

Key capabilities & outputs · click to inspect

Automated Quality Control: Inspect outliers, read or spectrum quality, and sample integrity across genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics and phenomics.

Variance & Batch Normalization: Assess variance-stabilizing transformations and batch-effect models while checking the preservation of biological variation.

Mixed-Effects & GLM Modeling: Generalized linear and mixed models tailored to experimental hierarchies, longitudinal measurements and genotype × environment interactions.

FDR & Multiple Testing Control: Account for multiple testing, effect sizes and uncertainty when selecting candidates for further investigation.

STAGE 03 · INTEGRATIVE AI & NETWORKSTop-Right Purple-Magenta Arc & Neural Nodes

Pattern Discovery & Predictive Modeling

Investigate biological relationships, regulatory networks and potential predictive biomarkers using machine learning and integrative computational methods.

Key capabilities & outputs · click to inspect

Co-Expression & Regulatory Networks: Explore gene–metabolite–phenotype modules to form hypotheses about regulators and pathways.

Predictive Biomarker Discovery: Compare ensemble and regularized models with cross-validation and assess the stability of predictive signatures for yield, resilience or efficacy.

Multi-Omics Latent Integration: Explore joint factors across genotype, microbiome, transcript and environmental layers through dimensionality reduction.

In-Silico Perturbation Ranking: Use feature attribution and perturbation models to prioritize hypotheses that still require experimental validation.

Actionable Results & Reproducible Delivery

Why AidBio exists

Make complex scientific data useful together.

Scientific projects bring together experiments, field observations, published knowledge, and computational results. When those pieces stay scattered, useful questions become harder to answer. AidBio connects biological context, analysis, and software so teams can trace the evidence, find insights, and shape the next question.
