What we do

Data work for people who study living systems.

We build models and tools around how biological and field data actually behave, not around generic templates.

Agriculture and climate

Weather and field data you can use

Field sensors fail, satellite images get clouded over and the nearest weather station may be far away. We combine your trial data with public climate records, fill the gaps carefully and compute measures like growing-degree days.

Reports and literature

Reports built from your data

Validation

Testing that matches real use

A random split of the data often puts near-identical samples, such as clones, replicates or neighbouring plots, on both sides of the test. The model looks great and then fails on new material. We split by field, genotype or experiment, so the score you see is the score you can expect.

Random split

Similar samples appear in both training and test data, so scores look better than they should.

Grouped split

Whole fields, clones or trials are held out, giving a realistic estimate for new data.
